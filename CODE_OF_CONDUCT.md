# Code of Conduct

This project is for people who build and publish browser extensions, and for
anyone who wants to help improve this tool. Everyone is welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal
  attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, hate speech, threats, spam, or deliberately disruptive
conduct in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, issues, and pull requests,
and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's
"Report content" option on the item, or open an issue that says you want to
raise a conduct concern without including details; the maintainer will arrange
a private way to talk.
